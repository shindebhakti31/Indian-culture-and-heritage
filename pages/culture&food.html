THE TASTE OF INDIA

Where Every Dish Tells a Story

Indian food is a beautiful reflection of the country's diverse cultures, traditions and history. Every region has its own ingredients, flavours, cooking techniques and traditional recipes. From spicy curries and delicious sweets to aromatic rice dishes and freshly prepared breads, Indian cuisine celebrates the richness of local ingredients and the joy of sharing food.

MORE THAN JUST FOOD

Food & Indian Traditions

Food is an important part of Indian festivals, family gatherings and everyday cultural traditions.

✦ Maharashtra

Maharashtrian food includes a wide variety of traditional dishes such as puran poli, bhakri, misal pav and modak. Modak is especially associated with Ganesh Chaturthi celebrations.

✦ Punjab

Punjabi food culture celebrates hearty meals, traditional breads and dairy-based dishes. Makki di roti and sarson da saag are especially popular during the winter season.

✦ Gujarat

Gujarati food traditions include a variety of vegetarian dishes, snacks and sweets. Dhokla, thepla and undhiyu are enjoyed in homes and during festive gatherings.

✦ Bengal

Bengali cuisine is known for its fish preparations, rice-based meals and traditional sweets. Festivals and family celebrations often feature dishes such as mishti doi and various pithas.

✦ Rajasthan

Rajasthani cuisine features traditional dishes shaped by the region's climate and local ingredients. Dal baati churma and gatte ki sabzi are among its well-known culinary specialities.

✦ South India

South Indian food traditions include rice, lentils, coconut and a variety of spices. Idli, dosa, sambar and regional rice dishes are enjoyed as part of everyday meals and celebrations.

❝

Indian food is not just about flavours; it is a celebration of culture, tradition and togetherness.
