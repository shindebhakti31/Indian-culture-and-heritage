THE SPIRIT OF INDIA

Where Every Festival Tells a Story

India is a land of diverse cultures, traditions and celebrations. From the sparkling lights of Diwali to the vibrant colours of Holi, every festival carries a unique story of faith, happiness, gratitude and togetherness. These celebrations bring families and communities closer and reflect the rich heritage of India.

BEYOND THE CELEBRATION

Stories Behind the Festivals

✦ Diwali

Diwali is associated with different traditions across India, including the return of Lord Rama to Ayodhya in many Hindu traditions. The lighting of lamps symbolises hope, knowledge and the triumph of good.

✦ Holi

Holi is linked with stories of Prahlad and Holika and the playful traditions of Radha and Krishna. The festival celebrates renewal, joy and togetherness.

✦ Ganesh Chaturthi

Dedicated to Lord Ganesha, the festival is especially celebrated with great enthusiasm in Maharashtra. Community celebrations include prayers, music and traditional processions.

✦ Navratri

Navratri honours the divine feminine in different forms. In Gujarat, Garba and Dandiya Raas are important parts of the celebrations.

✦ Durga Puja

Durga Puja is particularly significant in West Bengal. It celebrates Goddess Durga's victory over Mahishasura and showcases remarkable artistic pandals and rituals.

✦ Eid

Eid al-Fitr marks the end of Ramadan, while Eid al-Adha commemorates devotion and sacrifice. Both are celebrated with prayers, charity and family gatherings.

✦ Makar Sankranti

Celebrated in different forms across India, this harvest festival is known as Uttarayan in Gujarat, Pongal in Tamil Nadu's seasonal celebrations and Magh Bihu in Assam.

✦ Onam

Onam celebrates Kerala's cultural heritage and the legend of King Mahabali. Pookalam, Onam Sadya and traditional performances are central to the festivities.
